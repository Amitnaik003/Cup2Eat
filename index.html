Good questions

            
Before the first bite.

            
Product details can change. Confirm current specifications with Cup2Eat before ordering or serving.

          

          

            

              What exactly is Cup2Eat?+
              
Cup2Eat is an edible cup designed to become part of the serving experience. Ask the team for current ingredients, formats, flavours, and product specifications.

            

            

              What can I serve in it?+
              
Potential uses include selected drinks, desserts, ice cream, tasting portions, and event serves. Suitability depends on the product variant and serving conditions.

            

            

              How long does it stay crisp?+
              
Performance can vary with temperature, filling, serving time, and storage. Request current product guidance for verified information.

            

            

              What ingredients or allergens does it contain?+
              
Always check the current product specification or packaging for ingredients and allergen information before purchase and service.

            

            

              Can I order samples or buy in bulk?+
              
Cup2Eat welcomes business enquiries. Share your location, business type, expected quantities, and intended use to discuss current options.

            

            

              Can the cups be customised?+
              
Ask the team about currently available formats, packaging, and customisation possibilities.
